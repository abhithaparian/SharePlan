YOUR TURN-BY-TURN DAY

Every stop.
In exactly the right order.

Leave Jersey City at 1:30 PM. Take in the summit, enjoy a quick lunch at the historic Bear Mountain Inn, browse Cold Spring with coffee or a sweet treat, and finish with an elegant dinner before heading home. Each step has a navigation link.

01
1:30 PM · Drive

Leave Jersey City

Hit the road. Use live Google Maps routing rather than committing to a particular highway; Hudson Valley Saturday traffic can be unpredictable.

Good to know: Set your first destination to Perkins Memorial Tower. You should aim to arrive around 3:00–3:20 PM, but allow for delays.

Part I · The mountains
02
~3:00–3:15 PM · Scenic drive

Perkins Memorial Drive

Follow posted signs onto Perkins Memorial Drive and take the winding paved road toward the summit. The foliage from the car is part of the experience.

Good to know: The road is seasonal and weather/traffic dependent. If closed, skip straight to Hessian Lake rather than hiking up.

Drive to Perkins Tower ↗Park access alerts ↗
03
~3:15–3:50 PM · Must-do · viewpoint

Perkins Memorial Tower overlook

Park legally near the summit. Walk to the stone tower area and viewing platform for Hudson River, Bear Mountain Bridge, and autumn ridge views. Take your best photos here.

Good to know: Keep this as the priority stop. Avoid a strenuous hike; save walking energy for Cold Spring.

Summit navigation ↗
04
~4:00–4:20 PM · Late lunch / substantial snack · PRIORITY

Hiker’s Café · inside Bear Mountain Inn

Head down from the summit to the historic Bear Mountain Inn and grab a sandwich, salad, and a drink. This is your actual food stop before Cold Spring—quick enough to preserve the afternoon, satisfying enough to hold you until dinner.

Where: First floor of Bear Mountain Inn, just inside the east entrance near the parking kiosk. Published hours: Lunch until 5:00 PM (April–December). This is a casual grab-and-go café, not an elaborate sit-down lunch.

Navigate to Hiker’s Café ↗Menu concept & hours ↗
05
~4:20–4:30 PM · Optional 10-minute stroll

Hessian Lake

After the café, look out across Hessian Lake immediately by the Inn. Walk just the nearby waterfront section for reflections and colorful trees; there is no need to circle the lake.

Skip if behind: The summit, food, Cold Spring boutiques, and dinner take priority. Don’t spend 25 minutes on this optional walk.

Part II · The village
06
~4:30–5:00 PM · Scenic drive

Bear Mountain Bridge → Cold Spring

Head across Bear Mountain Bridge to the eastern bank, then drive north toward Cold Spring. The river crossing is a beautiful in-car moment.

Good to know: Keep eyes on the road; stop only in designated parking areas.

Cold Spring parking ↗
07
~5:00–5:05 PM · Park once

Park once · 49 Fair Street

Try the municipal parking lot on Fair Street. It is a paid visitor lot; follow the meter instructions and posted limits.

Good to know: If full, follow posted public parking signs rather than circling repeatedly. Your entire village visit is walkable.

Walk Main Street in this order
08
~5:05–5:18 PM · Coffee / sweet treat + boutique

Paulette · 114 Main Street

Your café stop in Cold Spring: order an espresso, cappuccino or small pastry if available, and browse the stylish fashion, accessories and home décor. Keep it leisurely but brief—dinner is ahead.

Published Saturday hours: 8 AM–6 PM. Paulette has a café/wine-bar concept; pastry selection and café service may vary late in the day.

09
~5:18–5:30 PM · Curated gifts

Cold Spring General Store · 66 Main Street

Continue west along Main Street to the curated local-goods store. Look for a gift, candle, ceramic or something charming to remember the day.

Good to know: Listed Saturday hours: 10 AM–6:30 PM. Roughly 10–15 minutes here is enough.

~5:30–5:43 PM · Waterfront + photos

Cold Spring waterfront

Walk down Main Street toward the Hudson waterfront. Take in the mountain-and-river scenery, get a few photos, and head back to the parked car.

Timing: Sunset is around 5:56 PM. Leave time to reach your dinner destination.

Part III · Dinner & return
11
~5:43–6:20 PM · Drive to dinner

Drive to The Abbey Inn

Get back to your car and head to 900 Fort Hill Road, Peekskill. Allow extra time for the river-valley roads and parking.

Good to know: Do not depend on a 6:30 table unless you have reserved one.

Navigate to The Abbey Inn ↗
12
~6:20–6:30 PM · Check out the setting

The Abbey Inn grounds

Arrive at the restored historic abbey. If timing and access permit, take a quick look at the grounds and step inside for an elegant date-night atmosphere.

Good to know: Keep this short if you are running late; the restaurant is the destination.

13
~6:30–8:00 PM · Proper dinner · the finale

Apropos Restaurant & Bar

Settle in for a relaxed, upscale contemporary American dinner at Apropos. Browse the menu, choose a starter and entrées, and enjoy the atmosphere at The Abbey Inn. Drinks are optional—make sure the driver stays alcohol-free.

Reservation: 6:30 PM for two is a suggested time, NOT confirmed. Saturday dinner is listed 5–9 PM. If you have not booked, call or check the reservation page before leaving Cold Spring.

Drive home to Jersey City

Head back to Jersey City after dinner; a reasonable rough arrival range is 9:15–10:00 PM, depending on traffic and your dinner end time.
