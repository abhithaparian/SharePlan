Times are flexible estimates

We leave Jersey City at 1:30 PM and target the summit around 3:00–3:20 PM. Saturday foliage traffic can push arrival later; treat every listed time as approximate. There's no long hike planned—this day is about gorgeous views, wandering through a storybook town, and an excellent dinner.

3:00–4:20 PM
STOP 01 · THE VIEWS

Bear Mountain

SCENIC

Prioritize Perkins Memorial Drive and its summit overlook (if open). The overlook near Perkins Memorial Tower is the signature view of the day. If time permits, head down to Hessian Lake for a brief waterfront walk and photos.

  • Best moment: the sweeping panorama of the Hudson River and fiery autumn hills.
  • Don't overdo it: 35–45 minutes at the summit, 15–20 minutes by the lake; skip strenuous trails.
  • Practical: Perkins Memorial Drive is seasonal and weather permitting; park entry and parking depend on capacity. Official park phone: (845) 786-2701.

STOP 02 · THE CHARM

Cold Spring

BOUTIQUES

Cross the Bear Mountain Bridge and follow the route to Cold Spring. For straightforward parking, try the paid municipal lot at 49 Fair Street, then stroll west along Main Street. Park once, then spend about 45–50 minutes on Main Street—your shopping and café stop, not a shopping marathon.

  • Paulette · 114 Main St: chic fashion and homewares, plus a café and wine bar. Saturday closing: 6 PM.
  • Cold Spring General Store · 66 Main St: carefully selected local gifts, pantry finds and home goods. Saturday closing: 6:30 PM.
  • Last few minutes: stroll west to the riverfront for the Hudson Highlands view. Skip it if shopping runs late.

STOP 03 · THE FINALE

Apropos at The Abbey Inn

LUXURY DINING

End the day on a high note: Apropos is set inside The Abbey Inn, a beautifully restored historic property above the Hudson. Expect contemporary American cooking, cocktails, and polished date-night ambience.

  • Timing: leave Cold Spring by ~5:40 PM and allow time for the drive and arrival. Aim for a 6:30 PM table.
  • Reservation: not yet confirmed. Reserve for two before committing to this stop or call (914) 739-3546.
  • Dress: smart casual / date night. You can enjoy cocktails together, but keep the drive home in mind.
